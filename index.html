About Us

A trusted Kampala-based company specialising in metal works and construction.

Quality Workmanship You Can Trust

Ayesiga Metal Works and Construction Services is a privately owned company based in Kampala, Uganda. Founded and led by Ayesiga Roberts, we provide professional welding, metal fabrication and construction services to residential, commercial and institutional clients.

From custom metal gates and structural steel to building works and renovations, we focus on delivering durable results, clear communication and work that stands the test of time.
