About Us

Learn more about Ayesiga Metal Works and Construction Services — a Kampala-based company built on skill, reliability and quality workmanship.

Our Story

Ayesiga Metal Works and Construction Services is a privately owned company based in Kampala, Uganda. Founded and led by Ayesiga Roberts, the business has grown through practical experience, consistent quality and a strong focus on client satisfaction.

What began as a metal fabrication and welding workshop has expanded into a full-service company offering structural steel works, custom metal products and general construction services.

Today we serve residential, commercial and institutional clients across Kampala and surrounding areas, delivering durable results and clear communication on every project.

Led by Experience

Ayesiga Roberts brings hands-on expertise in welding, metal fabrication and construction. With a practical approach and attention to detail, he ensures every project is completed to a high standard — from small custom gates to larger structural and building works.

The company remains independently owned and operated, focused on delivering reliable service and building long-term relationships with clients.

— Ayesiga Roberts, Founder
